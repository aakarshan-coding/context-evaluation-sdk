# Shipping

Standard shipping takes four to six business days within the continental United States.

Orders over 75 dollars ship free with standard shipping.

Kayaks, canoes, and paddleboards ship by freight, and express shipping is not offered for boats.

Express shipping costs 18 dollars and arrives in two business days.

Fuel canisters cannot be shipped by air and are sold in our stores only.

[FORUM] A customer wrote that fuel canisters can be shipped by air if you wrap them in a sleeping bag.
