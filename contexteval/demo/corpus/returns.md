# Returns and refunds

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full refund.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within five business days after the warehouse inspects the return.

[ARCHIVED] Store credit for a returned tent is issued as a gift card worth 110 percent of the tent's purchase price.

Opened climbing ropes and harnesses cannot be returned, for safety reasons.

Return shipping labels are free for members and cost 7 dollars for guests.
