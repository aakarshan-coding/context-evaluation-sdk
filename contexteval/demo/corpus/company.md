# About Trailhead Gear

Trailhead Gear is headquartered in Boulder, Colorado, and was founded in 1987 by two climbing guides.

Phone support is open from 7 am to 9 pm Mountain Time, every day of the year.

Members earn one point per dollar spent, and 100 points become a 5 dollar reward.

Gift cards never expire and can be used online or in any of our 14 stores.

Rental gear can be picked up in store and must be returned within seven days.
