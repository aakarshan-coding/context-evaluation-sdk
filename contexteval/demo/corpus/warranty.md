# Warranty

The warranty covers manufacturing defects in tents, packs, and sleeping bags for as long as you own the item.

How long the warranty lasts for a sleeping bag does not depend on where the sleeping bag was bought.

The warranty does not cover sleeping bags damaged by campfire sparks, pets, or a zipper forced closed.

To keep the warranty valid, store sleeping bags loose and never compressed in a stuff sack for long.

Sleeping bag warranty claims need a photo of the damage and the order number.

Down-filled sleepers carry a ten year guarantee against baffle and seam failure.

Repairs under warranty usually take three weeks, and we pay shipping both ways.
