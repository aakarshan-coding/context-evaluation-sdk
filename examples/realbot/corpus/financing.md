# Financing

We offer financing through Summit Pay, a third-party lender, subject to credit approval. Applying takes about five minutes in store or at checkout online and does a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score.

Purchases of $999 or more qualify for 0% APR for 12 months. Purchases of $2,500 or more qualify for 0% APR for 18 months. If you don't pay the full balance by the end of the promotional period, interest is charged at the standard rate from that point on, not back to the purchase date.

Standard financing for purchases between $250 and $999 is available at 9.99% to 29.99% APR for 6 to 36 months, depending on credit.

Financing can be used on bikes, parts, and accessories. It can't be used for service, rentals, or gift cards.
