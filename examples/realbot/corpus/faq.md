# Frequently Asked Questions

## Buying a bike

**Can I test ride a bike before buying?**
Yes. Test rides are free at both stores. Bring a photo ID; we hold it while you ride. Test rides on the Trailhawk Pro and e-bikes need a credit card on file as well. Test rides are up to 30 minutes on the streets around the store.

**Do bikes come with pedals?**
Road, gravel, and mountain bikes usually don't include pedals, because most riders have a preference. Our city bikes, the Metro-E, the Metro-E Cargo, and all Sprout kids' bikes come with flat pedals. Basic flat pedals are $25 if you need a pair.

**Do you assemble bikes bought in store?**
Every bike we sell in store is assembled and safety checked by a mechanic before you take it home. Assembly is free.

**Can I order a bike that isn't in stock?**
Yes. Special orders need a 20% deposit. The deposit is refundable until the bike ships from the distributor.

**Do you sell used bikes?**
We sell some used bikes that came in through trade-ins. Used bikes are inspected and serviced, and come with a 90-day warranty on the frame and parts. They aren't covered by the New Bike Service Plan.

## Gift cards

**Do gift cards expire?**
No. Ridgeline gift cards never expire and have no fees. They can be used at both stores and online, for anything including service and rentals. Lost gift cards can be replaced if you have the receipt.

## Service

**Do I need an appointment for service?**
No, you can drop off any time the service department is open. Appointments guarantee a start date, which helps in spring. Book online.

**Will you work on a bike I bought somewhere else?**
Yes. We service every brand, including e-bikes from other brands as long as the motor maker's diagnostic software is available to shops. We can't service e-bikes with hub motors from brands that don't sell parts to shops.

**Can you install parts I bought online?**
Yes, at our normal labor rates. We can't warranty the part itself, only our work.

**How often should I service my bike?**
For most riders, a Basic Tune-Up every 6 months and a Full Tune-Up once a year is plenty. Mountain bikes ridden hard need suspension service as well.

## Tubeless and tires

**Should I go tubeless?**
For mountain and gravel riding, yes. Tubeless lets you run lower pressure with fewer flats. You need to top up sealant every 3 to 6 months, more often in hot, dry weather.

**What tire pressure should I run?**
It depends on your weight, tire width, and terrain. As a starting point, gravel tires at 40c run 35 to 45 psi, and 29 x 2.4 inch mountain tires run 20 to 26 psi with tubeless. Road tires at 28c run 60 to 80 psi.

## Electric bikes

**Where can I ride an e-bike?**
In Colorado, Class 1 and Class 2 e-bikes are allowed on most bike paths. Many open-space trails in Boulder County allow Class 1 e-bikes only, and some ban e-bikes entirely. Check the rules for each trail before you ride.

**Can I add a throttle to my Metro-E?**
No. Adding a throttle would make it a Class 2 e-bike and voids the electrical warranty.

**Do I need a license or insurance for an e-bike?**
Not in Colorado for Class 1, 2, or 3 e-bikes. We recommend checking whether your renter's or homeowner's insurance covers theft.

## Other

**Do you offer discounts for students or teachers?**
We offer 10% off accessories and service for students and teachers with a valid ID. The discount doesn't apply to bikes or combine with Ridgeline Rewards.

**Can I bring my dog to the store?**
Leashed, well-behaved dogs are welcome in both stores.
