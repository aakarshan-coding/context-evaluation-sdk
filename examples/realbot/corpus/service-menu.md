# Service Menu and Pricing

Our mechanics work on every kind of bike, including brands we don't sell. Prices below are for labor; parts are extra.

## Tune-ups

- Basic Tune-Up, $89: adjust gears and brakes, true wheels while on the bike, check bolts, lube the chain.
- Full Tune-Up, $159: everything in the Basic, plus wheels trued off the bike, bearing adjustment, drivetrain degrease, and cable replacement if needed (cables extra).
- Overhaul, $299: complete teardown, every bearing cleaned or replaced, frame cleaned, and the bike rebuilt. Recommended once a year for bikes ridden more than 3,000 miles a year.

## E-bike service

E-bike diagnostic, $65: we connect to the motor system, read error codes, check battery health, and update firmware. If you go ahead with the repair, the diagnostic fee is credited toward it. An e-bike tune-up is $119; it is a Basic Tune-Up plus the diagnostic.

## Common repairs

- Flat repair, $15 plus the tube, or $25 for a tubeless plug and sealant top-up
- Brake bleed, $45 per brake
- Wheel true, $30 per wheel
- Tubeless setup, $40 per wheel, sealant included

## Turnaround

Most work is finished in 3 to 5 business days. In spring (March through June) expect 5 to 8 business days. Rush service is available for a $40 surcharge and gets your bike done within one business day when the parts are in stock.

We store finished bikes for 14 days after we tell you they're ready. After that we charge $5 a day for storage.
