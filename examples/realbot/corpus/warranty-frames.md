# Ridgeline Frame Warranty

This page covers frames and forks on Ridgeline house-brand bikes. Bikes from other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty, and we handle the claim for you if you bought the bike from us.

## Aluminum and steel frames

Ridgeline aluminum and steel frames carry a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship for the original owner. Proof of purchase is required. The warranty is not transferable.

## Carbon frames

Ridgeline carbon frames are warranted for 7 years from the date of purchase for the original owner. After the warranty ends, we offer a crash replacement discount of 40% off a replacement frame for the life of the bike.

## Forks and rear shocks

Suspension forks and rear shocks are covered by their manufacturer, usually for 2 years. Rigid forks that ship with a Ridgeline frame carry the same warranty as the frame.

## What isn't covered

The frame warranty does not cover damage from crashes, impacts, improper assembly, neglect, or use outside the bike's intended category. Paint, decals, and normal wear are not covered. Frames used for commercial rental or delivery work are covered for 2 years only, regardless of material.

## How to make a claim

Bring the bike to either store. We inspect the frame, photograph the damage, and submit the claim. Most frame claims are decided within 10 business days. If a claim is approved and the same frame is no longer made, we replace it with the closest current model.
