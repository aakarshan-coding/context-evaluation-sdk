# Returns and Exchanges

We want you to love what you ride. If something isn't right, you can return or exchange most items. Bring your receipt or order number; returns without proof of purchase are refunded as store credit at the lowest recent selling price.

## Bikes

Non-electric bikes can be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund if they have been ridden fewer than 50 miles and show no damage. A bike that has been ridden more than that can still be exchanged for a different model within 30 days, but not refunded.

## Electric bikes

E-bikes can be returned within 14 days of purchase. Returned e-bikes are subject to a 15% restocking fee, because we have to recertify the battery before resale. The restocking fee is waived if you exchange the e-bike for another e-bike of equal or greater value.

## Parts, accessories, and clothing

Unused parts, accessories, and clothing in original packaging can be returned within 60 days. Helmets can only be returned if the inner liner sticker is intact. Installed parts, custom-cut items (bar tape, cables, cut steerer tubes), and tires that have been mounted can't be returned.

## Online orders

Online orders follow the same time limits, counted from the delivery date. Return shipping for accessories is free using the label in your account. For a shipped bike return, we deduct a $75 return shipping charge from the refund unless the bike arrived damaged or was the wrong item.

## Sale and clearance items

Items marked final sale can't be returned or exchanged. Other sale items follow the normal policy.
