# Sprout Kids' Bikes

Sprout is our line of kids' bikes. They are lighter than most kids' bikes, so children can handle them more easily.

The Sprout 16 has 16-inch wheels, fits children about 4 to 6 years old (roughly 39 to 46 inches tall), weighs 6.8 kg, and costs $329. It comes with removable training wheels and a coaster brake plus a rear hand brake.

The Sprout 20 has 20-inch wheels, fits children about 6 to 9 years old (roughly 45 to 54 inches tall), weighs 8.1 kg, and costs $429. It has two hand brakes and a 7-speed drivetrain.

The Sprout 24 has 24-inch wheels, fits children about 8 to 12 years old, weighs 9.9 kg, and costs $499. It has a 9-speed drivetrain and an air suspension fork.

## Sprout Trade-Up

Kids grow fast. When your child outgrows a Sprout bought from us, trade it in toward the next size up and get 50% of the original purchase price as credit. The trade-up is available within 3 years of the original purchase, and the bike must be in rideable condition. Trade-up credit can only be used on another Sprout bike.
