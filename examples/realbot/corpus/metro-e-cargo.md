# Metro-E Cargo

The Metro-E Cargo is a longtail cargo e-bike for carrying kids, groceries, or work gear. It is longer and heavier than the Metro-E and needs more room to park and turn.

## Motor and battery

The Cargo uses a 250 W mid-drive motor with 85 Nm of torque, tuned for heavy loads. Like the Metro-E it is Class 1, with assistance up to 20 mph and no throttle. It comes with one 625 Wh battery and has a mount for a second, optional 625 Wh battery.

With one battery, expect 30 to 60 miles of range; with two, 60 to 110 miles. Charging a single battery from empty takes about 6 hours with the included charger.

## Load limits

The maximum total payload, including the rider, is 200 kg (440 lb). The rear rack alone is rated for 80 kg (176 lb). Two child seats fit on the rear deck, or one child seat plus a cargo bag.

## Specs

- Frame: aluminum longtail, one size fits riders 5'0" to 6'4"
- Drivetrain: Shimano Cues 1x11
- Brakes: four-piston hydraulic disc, 203 mm rotors
- Tires: 20 x 2.6 inch rear, 24 x 2.4 inch front
- Weight: 35 kg (77 lb) with one battery
- Price: $3,999

A second 625 Wh battery costs $799. The Cargo does not fit on most hitch-mounted bike racks.
