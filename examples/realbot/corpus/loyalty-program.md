# Ridgeline Rewards

Ridgeline Rewards is our free loyalty program. Sign up in store or online with your email address.

Members earn 1 point for every dollar spent on bikes, parts, accessories, and clothing, and 2 points for every dollar spent on service. Rentals and gift card purchases don't earn points.

Every 500 points turns into a $25 reward certificate, which is emailed to you automatically. Reward certificates expire 90 days after they are issued. Points themselves expire if your account has no purchases for 18 months.

Members also get 10% off labor on service at both stores, early access to our spring and fall sales, and a free flat repair (labor only) once per calendar year.

Points can't be combined across accounts, and they are not earned on the portion of a purchase paid with a reward certificate.
