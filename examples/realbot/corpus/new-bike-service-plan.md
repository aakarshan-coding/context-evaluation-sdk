# New Bike Service Plan

Every new bike bought from Ridgeline Cycles includes our New Bike Service Plan at no charge.

For the first 12 months after purchase, we'll do free adjustments as often as you need them: shifting, brake rub, headset play, and spoke tension. New cables stretch, so we suggest coming in after your first 100 miles.

The plan also includes one free Basic Tune-Up, which must be used within 6 months of purchase. E-bikes get the e-bike tune-up instead.

The plan covers labor only. Parts and wear items, flat repairs, and crash damage are not included. The plan stays with the bike if you sell it within the 12 months; the new owner just needs the original receipt.

Bikes bought online and shipped to you are also covered, at either store.
