# Rentals

You can rent bikes from our Boulder store. Fort Collins does not rent bikes.

## Rates

| Bike | Half day (4 hours) | Full day | Each extra day |
|---|---|---|---|
| City hybrid | $30 | $45 | $35 |
| Trailhawk 29 hardtail | $50 | $75 | $60 |
| Trailhawk Pro full suspension | $80 | $120 | $95 |
| Metro-E e-bike | $60 | $95 | $75 |

Rentals include a helmet, a lock, a flat kit, and a pump. Pedals are included; you can bring your own clipless pedals and we'll install them free.

## Requirements

Renters must be 18 or older, or 14 or older with a parent or guardian signing the waiver. We need a valid photo ID and a credit card for a security deposit. The deposit is $250 for non-electric bikes and $500 for e-bikes.

## Reservations and cancellations

Reserve online or by phone. Cancel at least 24 hours ahead for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours are charged 50% of the first day. Full-day rentals must be returned by 6 pm, or by 5 pm on Sundays.

If you buy a new bike within 30 days of renting the same model, we credit up to two days of rental fees toward the purchase.
