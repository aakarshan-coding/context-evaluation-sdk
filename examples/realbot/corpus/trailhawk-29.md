# Trailhawk 29 Hardtail

The Trailhawk 29 is our aluminum hardtail mountain bike for people getting into trail riding or who want one bike that climbs well and stays simple to maintain.

## Specs

- Frame: 6061 aluminum, internal dropper post routing
- Fork: RockShox Judy Silver, 120 mm travel
- Drivetrain: Shimano Deore 1x12, 30t chainring, 10-51t cassette
- Brakes: Shimano MT410 hydraulic disc, 180 mm rotors front and rear
- Wheels: 29 inch, tubeless ready
- Tires: 29 x 2.4 inch, maximum tire clearance 2.6 inch
- Weight: 13.6 kg (30.0 lb), size medium, without pedals
- Sizes: S, M, L, XL
- Price: $1,349

## Fit

Small fits riders from about 5'2" to 5'6", medium from 5'6" to 5'10", large from 5'10" to 6'1", and XL from 6'1" to 6'5". If you are between sizes, we suggest a test ride on both.

Pedals are not included. The bike ships with a rigid seatpost, and a 125 mm dropper is a $229 upgrade at purchase.
