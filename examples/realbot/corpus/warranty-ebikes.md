# E-Bike Warranty

Our electric bikes (the Metro-E and the Metro-E Cargo) have a separate warranty for their electrical system. The frame itself is covered by the Ridgeline frame warranty.

## Coverage periods

The drive motor is covered for 3 years from the date of purchase. The battery is covered for 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. The display, controller, and wiring harness are covered for 2 years. The charger is covered for 1 year.

A battery is considered defective if it holds less than 70% of its rated capacity within the coverage period. Normal capacity loss above that line is not a defect.

## Cargo bikes used for work

If a Metro-E Cargo is used for commercial delivery, the motor coverage drops to 18 months and the battery coverage to 12 months.

## What voids the e-bike warranty

Opening the battery case, using a charger we didn't supply, installing a speed tuning device, or modifying the controller firmware voids the electrical warranty. Water damage from submerging the bike or pressure washing the motor is not covered.

## Claims

Bring the bike, the charger, and the battery key to our Boulder store. We run a diagnostic and contact the motor maker if needed. Warranty diagnostics are free; if the problem turns out not to be covered, we charge the standard e-bike diagnostic fee.
