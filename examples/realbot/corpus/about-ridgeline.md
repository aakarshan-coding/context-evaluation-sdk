# About Ridgeline Cycles

Ridgeline Cycles is an independent bike shop founded in 2009 by Dana Okafor and Luis Ferreira. We started as a two-person repair stand in a garage off Pearl Street and now run two stores in northern Colorado. We sell road, gravel, mountain, kids, and electric bikes, and we design a small line of house-brand bikes and parts.

## Stores and hours

Our Boulder store is at 1840 Folsom Street. It is open Monday through Friday from 10 am to 7 pm, Saturday from 9 am to 6 pm, and Sunday from 11 am to 5 pm. The Boulder service department closes one hour before the store each day.

Our Fort Collins store is at 312 Linden Street in Old Town. It is open Tuesday through Saturday from 10 am to 6 pm and is closed Sunday and Monday. Fort Collins does not do e-bike motor or battery repairs; those jobs are sent to Boulder and usually add two business days.

Both stores are closed on Thanksgiving, December 25, and January 1. On the Fourth of July we close at 2 pm.
