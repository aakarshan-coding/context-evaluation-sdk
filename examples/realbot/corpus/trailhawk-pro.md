# Trailhawk Pro Full Suspension

The Trailhawk Pro is a carbon full-suspension trail bike built for long days and technical descents. It shares a name with the Trailhawk 29 but almost nothing else.

## Specs

- Frame: full carbon front and rear triangle
- Rear travel: 140 mm, Fox Float X shock
- Fork: Fox 36 Performance, 150 mm travel
- Drivetrain: SRAM GX Eagle Transmission 1x12, 32t chainring
- Brakes: SRAM Code R four-piston, 200 mm front rotor and 180 mm rear rotor
- Wheels: 29 inch front and rear (a mixed-wheel 27.5 rear option is available in sizes S and M)
- Tires: 29 x 2.5 inch front, 29 x 2.4 inch rear
- Dropper post: 170 mm on M and larger, 150 mm on S
- Weight: 14.2 kg (31.3 lb), size medium, without pedals
- Price: $4,199

## Ownership notes

We recommend a suspension service on the rear shock every 50 riding hours and a full fork rebuild every 125 hours. Our shop does lower-leg services in house; full damper rebuilds are sent to Fox and take about two weeks.

The Trailhawk Pro is not rated for bike park or downhill use. Riding it on lift-served downhill trails is outside its intended category for warranty purposes.
