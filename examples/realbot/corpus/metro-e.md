# Metro-E Commuter E-Bike

The Metro-E is a pedal-assist commuter built for daily rides to work, school, or the grocery store. It comes with fenders, a rear rack, and integrated lights.

## Motor and battery

The Metro-E uses a 250 W mid-drive motor with 65 Nm of torque. It is a Class 1 e-bike: the motor assists up to 20 mph and has no throttle. The removable 500 Wh battery locks into the down tube and charges on or off the bike.

Range depends on assist level, rider weight, terrain, and temperature. Expect 25 to 55 miles per charge. A full charge from empty takes about 4.5 hours with the included 4 A charger.

## Specs

- Frame: aluminum, step-through or diamond
- Drivetrain: Shimano Cues 1x9
- Brakes: hydraulic disc, 180 mm rotors
- Tires: 700 x 45c with puncture protection
- Rack capacity: 25 kg (55 lb)
- Maximum total weight (rider plus cargo): 136 kg (300 lb)
- Weight: 23.5 kg (51.8 lb) with battery
- Price: $2,399

## Accessories

A second 500 Wh battery costs $649. A front basket that mounts to the head tube is $89.
