# Shipping and Delivery

We ship to the 48 contiguous United States. We don't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, or outside the US.

## Accessories and parts

Orders of parts and accessories over $75 ship free by ground. Orders under $75 ship for a flat $8.95. Ground orders usually arrive in 3 to 6 business days. Two-day shipping is $24.95.

## Bikes

Bikes ship 90% assembled in a bike box; you install the handlebars, front wheel, and pedals. Shipping a non-electric bike costs $99. Bikes take 5 to 8 business days to arrive.

E-bikes ship by freight because of their lithium batteries. E-bike shipping costs $199 and takes 7 to 10 business days. Someone 18 or older must sign for the delivery. Spare e-bike batteries ship only to addresses in Colorado, Wyoming, Utah, New Mexico, Kansas, and Nebraska.

## Local delivery

Within 25 miles of either store, we deliver fully assembled bikes for $49, or free on bikes over $2,000. We schedule local delivery on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## Damaged shipments

If your box arrives damaged, photograph it before you open it and email the photos within 48 hours of delivery. We'll replace damaged parts or arrange a return at no charge.
