# Parts and Component Warranty

Most components on the bikes we sell (drivetrains, brakes, wheels, saddles) are made by other companies and are covered by those companies. Shimano and SRAM components are generally covered for 2 years. We process the claim for anything you bought from us.

Ridgeline house-brand components, such as our Switchback handlebars, Arc seatposts, and Loop saddles, are covered for 2 years. Ridgeline wheels are covered for 3 years, and the rims carry a one-time crash replacement at half price during that time.

Tires, tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes, cables, and grips are wear items. They are covered only for defects found within 30 days of purchase.

Labor is free for warranty work on parts we installed. If you installed the part yourself, we charge our normal labor rate for the swap.
