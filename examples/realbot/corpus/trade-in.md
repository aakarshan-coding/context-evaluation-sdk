# Adult Bike Trade-In

You can trade in your current bike toward a new one at either store. We appraise the bike on the spot, usually in about 20 minutes.

The trade-in value is based on the bike's age, condition, and what it would sell for used. We offer up to 40% of our used-resale appraisal as store credit toward a new bike. If you'd rather have cash, we don't buy bikes outright.

We accept bikes that are less than 10 years old and in working condition. We don't accept department-store bikes, bikes with cracked frames, or e-bikes with batteries that won't hold a charge. E-bikes are accepted only if they are less than 5 years old.

Trade-in credit is applied the same day and must be used toward a new bike, not parts or service. Only one trade-in can be applied per new bike.

Kids' Sprout bikes use the separate Sprout Trade-Up program instead.
