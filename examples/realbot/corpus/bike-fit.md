# Bike Fitting

A good fit makes a bike more comfortable and more efficient, and it can fix knee, back, and hand pain.

## Quick Fit

The Quick Fit takes about 30 minutes. We set saddle height, saddle setback, and handlebar reach using your measurements and a short ride on the trainer. It costs $60 and is free with any new bike purchase over $1,500.

## Pro Fit

The Pro Fit is a two-and-a-half-hour session with our certified fitter, Mara Lindqvist, at the Boulder store. It includes a flexibility assessment, cleat setup, saddle pressure mapping, and a written fit report you can take to any shop. It costs $225. If you buy a new bike within 60 days after a Pro Fit, we credit $100 of the fit fee toward the bike.

Parts swapped during a fit, such as stems, saddles, or spacers, are charged separately at 15% off.

## Booking

Book fits online. Pro Fits are scheduled on Wednesdays and Saturdays only. Bring your bike, your riding shoes, and the shorts you usually ride in.
