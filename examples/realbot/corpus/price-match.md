# Price Match Policy

If you find a lower price on an identical new item from an authorized local bike shop in Colorado, we'll match it. Show us the advertised price, in print or on the shop's website, at the time of purchase.

We also match the price on a manufacturer's own website for items they sell direct, such as Shimano, SRAM, or Fox parts.

If you buy something from us and our own price drops within 14 days, we'll refund the difference. Bring the receipt or order number.

We don't match online-only retailers, marketplaces like eBay or Amazon, used or open-box items, clearance or closeout prices, bundles, or prices that require a membership. Price matching does not apply to Ridgeline house-brand bikes, which we only sell ourselves.
